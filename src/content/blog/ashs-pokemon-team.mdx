# Every Pokemon Ash Catches in Pokemon Fire Ash – Full Team List by Region

If you’ve ever watched the Pokémon anime and wished you could take command of Ash’s journey yourself, **Pokemon Fire Ash** is the game that makes it happen. You don’t create a custom trainer – you *are* Ash Ketchum, traveling from Pallet Town through every region, collecting the exact Pokémon he caught in the show.

Below is a complete, region-by-region catalog of every Pokémon Ash obtains in Pokemon Fire Ash, based on the anime storyline. The game follows the show closely, but you have some freedom in how you build your team – you can choose to keep or release certain Pokémon, and you can even evolve some that Ash never did (like Gible). However, the story events that trigger captures are locked to the anime progression.

If you’re new to the game, check out our [Pokemon Fire Ash guide](/guide) for download and installation instructions.

## Kanto (Including Orange Islands)

Ash’s journey begins in Kanto with Pikachu, and he picks up a classic team along the way. The Orange Islands arc is included in this region.

### Kanto Team

| Pokemon | Catch location / Notes |
|---------|----------------------|
| Pikachu | Starter from Professor Oak in Pallet Town. Follows you on the overworld. |
| Caterpie | Viridian Forest – evolves into Metapod then Butterfree. You have the option to release Butterfree later. |
| Pidgeotto | Viridian Forest area – evolves into Pidgeot. You can release it or keep it. |
| Bulbasaur | Gift from the Cerulean City Pokémon Center after saving it from Team Rocket. |
| Charmander | Abandoned by its trainer near Route 24/25. |
| Squirtle | Joins after helping the Squirtle Squad in the Cerulean area. |
| Krabby | Caught with a Fishing Rod – evolves into Kingler. |
| Muk | Found in the wild during the Pokémon Lovers Club event. |
| Tauros | Safari Zone – Ash caught an entire herd of 30 Tauros in the anime. In the game you catch multiple, but one counts as your main. |
| Haunter | Given to Ash by Sabrina’s father in Lavender Town – eventually evolves into Gengar (though it was released in the anime). |
| Snorlax | Route 16 – you awaken and catch it. |
| Lapras | Gift from the Orange Islands – used for travel and battle. |
| Aerodactyl | Revived from an Old Amber in the Pewter City lab. |

**Orange Islands additions:** During the Orange Islands arc, Ash also catches a **Dratini** (event) and a few temporary team members, but his main captures are Lapras and Snorlax. The game includes the Orange League challenge.

## Johto

Ash’s Johto journey starts after the Orange Islands. He brings Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Squirtle, and Charizard with him, but catches several new Pokémon.

| Pokemon | Catch location / Notes |
|---------|----------------------|
| Heracross | Route 46 (early Johto) – used sparingly but appears in battles. |
| Chikorita | Gift from Professor Elm in New Bark Town. |
| Cyndaquil | Received from Professor Elm as his second starter. |

**Key Johto note:** The game also allows you to capture **Misty’s Gyarados** and **Brock’s Steelix** as temporary allies, but these are not permanent additions.

## Special Notes on Evolution and Story Lock

- **Trade evolutions** (e.g., Gengar, Machamp, Alakazam) are changed to evolve by level-up or using an item.
- **Ash-Greninja** transforms during story battles after bonding events. It’s a separate form, not a permanent evolution.
- **Pikachu** never evolves; it stays as Pikachu but learns new moves like Electroweb, Thunderbolt, and Iron Tail.
- Some Pokémon (like Goodra) can be recalled to the party later, but the game follows the anime’s releases (Butterfree, Lapras, etc.) as optional events.
- You can choose to not release certain Pokémon, but the main story events treat them as gone for cutscenes.

## How to Get the Full Experience

If you want to catch every Pokémon Ash ever owned, you need to play through all regions in order. The game saves your team across regions, so you can bring your favorites forward. However, some regions (like Hoenn and Sinnoh) expect you to use region-specific Pokémon for Gym Battles – the game doesn’t force you to box your old team, but the anime-accurate battles are balanced accordingly.
