## The top three

### 1. Pokemon Unbound

The clear pick for most players. A new region, a difficulty you can actually
tune, and modern quality-of-life on top of a FireRed base. It is the hack we
point newcomers to first.

### 2. Pokemon Radical Red

For people who thought the main games were too easy. It is mean, fair, and
packed with modern mechanics. Not your first hack, but maybe your favorite.

### 3. Pokemon Renegade Platinum

The definitive Sinnoh. Every Platinum issue addressed, every Pokemon
accessible. If you want a complete, balanced pair of Nuzlocke-ready games,
start here.
