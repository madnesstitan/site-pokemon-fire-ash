## What Is Pokemon Fire Ash?

Created by Reinhartmax and released in 2021, Pokemon Fire Ash is a complete fan game that has been updated regularly since launch. The current version is **3.7.1** (July 2026). It runs on PC and Android (via Joiplay) — no emulator needed. The game follows the anime’s story loosely, covering every major region from Kanto through the Journeys arc, including the Orange Islands and the Battle Frontier. The game is designed to be a single-player experience with a focus on exploration, battle, and collection. It offers over 50 gym battles, more than 900 Pokemon up to Generation 8, and a deep post-game that includes the Grandeur Club and the Pokemon World Coronation Series. For installation instructions, see our [download guide](/blog/download-pokemon-fire-ash).

## Region-by-Region Breakdown

The game follows the anime’s timeline, region by region, with key story beats and battles. For a comparison with other anime-inspired fan games, read our [Fire Ash vs other anime fan games](/pokemon-fire-ash-vs-other-anime-fan-games) guide.

### Kanto

You start in Pallet Town, choose your first Pokemon (Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle), and set off through Viridian Forest, Pewter City, Cerulean City, and Vermilion City. Battles against Brock, Misty, Lt. Surge, Erika, Koga, Sabrina, and Blaine are all included. The Indigo League conference, the battle against Gary, and the final match against Ritchie are present. Team Rocket’s antics appear throughout.

### Orange Islands

After the Indigo League, you travel to the Orange Islands, where you challenge the Orange Crew gym leaders: Cissy, Danny, Rudy, and Luana. The final battle against Drake for the Winner’s Trophy is included. This section also includes the episode where Ash catches Snorlax and the battle against the mysterious Mewtwo.

The Johto arc begins with the journey to the Johto League, including the Whirl Cup, the battle against the Kimono Girls, and the Bug-Catching Contest
