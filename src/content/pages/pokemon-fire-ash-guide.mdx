# Pokemon Fire Ash Guide – Features, Regions, and Everything You Need to Know

Pokemon Fire Ash is a fan-made RPG Maker XP game that lets you relive Ash Ketchum's entire journey from Kanto to Alola, including the Orange Islands and the Battle Frontier. With over 50 gyms, 900+ Pokemon up to generation 8, and mechanics like Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, and Dynamax, it's one of the most ambitious Pokemon fangames ever made. This guide covers everything you need to know before you start playing.

<Callout type="info" title="Game Status">
  The game is **completed** — the main story ends after you become the World Champion by defeating Leon in Galar. A postgame is available, including the Grandeur Club with extra challenges. The latest version as of this writing is **3.6 Part 2.1**, released October 23, 2025.
</Callout>

## What Is Pokemon Fire Ash?

Created by Reinhartmax using RPG Maker XP and Pokemon Essentials, Pokemon Fire Ash took about two years to complete and has been updated for over five years. The story follows the anime loosely: you travel through Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, and the Orange Islands, battling Ash's companions and rivals along the way. The game uses generation 3 graphics (FireRed/LeafGreen style) but includes Pokemon, moves, and items up to generation 8.

For a high-level overview of the game's key features and how to get started, visit the [Pokemon Fire Ash home page](/).

## Regions and Gyms

You visit **nine regions** in total, each with its own set of gyms. The game has over 50 gym battles — more than any official Pokemon game.

- **Kanto** – The starting region, based on the original anime. You'll face the standard 8 gyms plus the Orange Islands gyms (4 in total).
- **Orange Islands** – A unique set of gyms with different challenges (e.g., Sumo, Race, Riddle). You'll need to beat them to earn the Orange League trophy.
- **Johto** – 8 gyms, following Ash's Johto journey.
- **Hoenn** – 8 gyms, with some deviations from the anime.
- **Sinnoh** – 8 gyms, including the full Sinnoh League.
- **Unova** – 8 gyms, covering the Best Wishes series.
- **Kalos** – 8 gyms, leading up to the Kalos League finals.
- **Alola** – 4 trials and the Pokemon League (the anime's Alola League).
- **Galar** – 8 gyms and the Champion Cup, culminating in the Masters Eight tournament.

The game also includes the **Battle Frontier** (from Hoenn and Sinnoh) as a postgame feature.

## Pokemon and Battle Mechanics

- **National Dex** with over 900 Pokemon up to generation 8 (including Galar starters, regional variants, and legendaries).
- **Abilities and moves** up to generation 8.
- **Mega Evolution** – Requires a Mega Ring and the appropriate Mega Stone.
- **Z-Moves** – Available in Alola and beyond.
- **Dynamax and Gigantamax** – Used in Galar gym battles and the Champion Cup.
- **Ash-Greninja** – A special form that can be obtained during the Kalos arc.
- **Alola Forms** – Catchable in Alola.
- **No HMs** – Fly is replaced with a warping system at Pokemon Centers. Other HM moves are handled by items or overworld abilities.
- **Trade evolutions** – Pokemon that normally evolve by trade now evolve by level or by using an item (e.g., Kadabra evolves into Alakazam at a certain level, or with a Link Stone).
- **Pikachu follows you** on the overworld, just like in the anime.

## Features and Quality of Life

The game has seen many improvements over its updates. Here are the standout features from the latest version (3.6 Part 2.1):

- **Speed up toggle** – Now persists even after closing the game.
- **Weather effects** – Can be disabled in the overworld.
- **PC search function** – Find Pokemon by name in your boxes.
- **Common Candy** – Decreases a Pokemon's level by 1 (useful for evolving early or adjusting for gym level caps). Available after becoming Orange League Champion.
- **Bulk buying** – Game Corner items and other shops allow buying in multiples.
- **Sort items alphabetically** – Use the quick save key (default left Alt).
- **Rotom Phone** – Tells you where to go during the Journeys arc.
- **Move animations** – Updated with new custom animations for Battle Bond transformation and more.
- **Legendary battle sprites** – New sprites for legendary and Dynamax Pokemon in the Journeys arc.
- **Region-specific music** – Different battle themes for legendaries and encounters with Jessie and James.

The game also includes a built-in **cheats menu** (accessible on Android by swiping down from the top of the screen). You can use it to skip sections or get rare items, but be careful — it can break the game if used improperly.

## How to Download Pokemon Fire Ash

The game is free to download. You can get the latest version from Mediafire or Mega. The file is a .zip archive containing the RPG Maker XP game folder.

**System requirements:**
- **PC:** Windows (XP or later). You may need to install **RPG Maker VX Ace RTP** (or the XP RTP) if you get a missing file error.
- **Android:** JoiPlay + RPG Maker Plugin. See the [full download and setup guide](/how-to-download-pokemon-fire-ash) for step-by-step instructions.

<Callout type="warn" title="Download Safely">
  Always download from trusted sources like the official PokeCommunity thread or PokeHarbor. Avoid fake download links that ask for payment.
</Callout>
